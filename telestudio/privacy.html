01 Overview & Data Controller

This Privacy Policy governs the TeleStudio Pro application (Bundle / Application ID: com.mmp.telestudio), available on iOS, iPadOS, and Android.

TeleStudio Pro was built for investigative journalists, corporate executives, filmmakers, content creators, and educators whose drafts and unedited takes are confidential. We do not collect, monetize, transmit, or sell any of your personal data.

Data Controller Contact: puludisu.apps@gmail.com

02 Device Permissions Requested

To operate as a broadcast video recording studio and teleprompter, TeleStudio Pro requests the following system permissions (shown with their iOS usage-description keys; Android requests the equivalent runtime permissions — Camera, Microphone, and media access):

  • Camera (NSCameraUsageDescription): Used solely to provide real-time preview and capture high-resolution 4K/1080p video while you read your teleprompter script.
  • Microphone (NSMicrophoneUsageDescription): Used exclusively to record synchronized audio for your video takes and to power the real-time speech tracking engine.
  • Speech Recognition (NSSpeechRecognitionUsageDescription on iOS): Powers the Voice-Tracking feature using the platform's on-device speech recognizer (Apple SFSpeechRecognizer on iOS, the Android SpeechRecognizer on Android). Speech is processed offline directly on your device.
  • Photo / Media Library (NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription · Android media access): Required only when you choose to export finished takes directly to your Photos / Gallery.
  • Local Network (NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription on iOS): Used only when you launch the "Duo Remote" feature to establish a direct, peer-to-peer connection between your filming device and a remote control device on your local Wi-Fi. Android uses standard local networking and shows no separate prompt.
Security Guarantee: None of these permissions grant access to external cloud services. All operations remain confined to your device.

03 Complete Absence of Third-Party Trackers

TeleStudio Pro contains zero advertising or profiling frameworks:

  • No Ad Networks: No Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads, or similar commercial ad SDKs.
  • No Analytics Beacons: No Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, AppsFlyer, Mixpanel, or Amplitude.
  • No Advertising ID: We never request Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) or Google's Advertising ID (GAID).
  • No Account Required: You never need to enter an email address, password, or profile information to use the app.

04 In-App Purchases (Apple StoreKit & Google Play Billing)

TeleStudio Pro is sold as a single lifetime purchase managed directly by the platform store — Apple StoreKit on iOS and Google Play Billing on Android:

  • We never receive or store your credit card details, billing address, or store-account credentials.
  • Purchase verification and restoration are handled cryptographically by Apple's and Google's secure servers.
  • All purchase disputes or refund requests are governed by Apple's App Store or Google Play refund policies.

05 Duo Remote Architecture

The Duo Remote controller feature runs a temporary local HTTP/WebSocket server inside the app:

  • Communication is strictly confined to your local Wi-Fi subnet (LAN).
  • Connection requires a dynamic, randomized 4-digit PIN generated directly on the filming screen.
  • No traffic is routed through cloud relay servers, proxies, or external networks — the server is reachable only on your local network, never from the Internet, and is torn down when you close the app.

06 Data Retention & Erasure

Because all scripts, settings, and video files reside strictly in the local app container on your device:

  • You can delete individual scripts or video takes at any time within the app's gallery views.
  • Deleting the TeleStudio app from your device immediately and permanently deletes all locally stored data.
