Frequently Asked Questions

Does TeleStudio work completely offline without Internet?

Yes. TeleStudio Pro was engineered with an offline-first architecture. 4K video recording, speech recognition for voice tracking, local Duo Remote over LAN, and video export function flawlessly with zero cellular or Wi-Fi connection.

How does the wireless Duo Remote control work?

TeleStudio runs a local server on the filming device. With both devices on the same Wi-Fi network — or one sharing a Personal Hotspot — scan the QR code (or open the local URL) on a second phone, tablet, or laptop and enter the 4-digit PIN. On mobile devices with TeleStudio installed, the Controller tab scans the Studio QR code directly with the camera to pair and connect in one tap. Control play/pause, scroll speed, line jumps, blackout, and recording from the browser or the in-app pad. Bluetooth pedals and presentation clickers are supported too, with no network needed.

Are my scripts or camera recordings stored in any cloud?

No, never. Neither your scripts, your biometric voice, nor your video recordings ever leave your device. We do not operate any user database or cloud storage. Your data remains strictly in your device's local sandbox.

Which languages does TeleStudio support?

The full interface is localized in 9 languages — English, French, Spanish, German, Portuguese, Italian, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, and Korean. It follows your device language automatically, and the Duo web remote is localized too. You can also force a specific language in Settings.

If I switch to a new phone, how do I restore my purchase?

Your Lifetime Pro license is permanently tied to your store account — your Apple ID on iOS or your Google account on Android. On your new device, sign in with the same account and tap "Restore Purchases" in TeleStudio's settings to instantly reactivate all Pro capabilities with zero additional charges.
