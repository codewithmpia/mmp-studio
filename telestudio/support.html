Troubleshooting & User Guides

How do I fix or optimize AI Voice Tracking?

TeleStudio uses your device's on-device neural speech recognizer to advance the teleprompter based on your spoken words. If the script does not scroll with your voice:

  1. Open your phone's Settings app and tap TeleStudio (or Apps → TeleStudio on Android).
  2. Verify that Microphone (and, on iOS, Speech Recognition) permissions are switched ON.
  3. In the studio recording screen, tap the microphone badge to toggle from manual speed to Voice Tracking mode. Speak at a natural speaking volume.
How do I connect the wireless Duo Remote control?

The Duo Remote works over your local network — from any browser (zero install) or from the TeleStudio app's built-in touch pad:

  1. Ensure both the filming device and the remote controller (phone, tablet, Mac, or PC) are on the same Wi-Fi network, or connect the controller to the filming device's Personal Hotspot.
  2. In TeleStudio, tap the Duo Remote button on the studio screen to reveal a QR code, a local URL (e.g. http://192.168.1.50:8080), and a 4-digit PIN.
  3. Browser: scan the QR code or open the URL on the second device and enter the PIN.
  4. In-app: on the second device, open the Remote → Controller tab and tap "Scanner le QR Code du Studio" to pair instantly with the camera (or expand manual entry to type the IP and PIN).
  5. Control scroll speed, play/pause, line jumps, blackout, and recording live.

Bluetooth remotes: presentation clickers and pedals also work with no network at all — pair them to the filming device, then use their Up/Down/Page/Space keys to drive play/pause, speed, and line jumps.

Connection refused? The two devices are on the network but the studio isn't serving on that port — make sure the filming device is foregrounded on the Studio or Remote screen, use the exact address/PIN shown there, and (on iOS) allow Local Network access in Settings.

How do I restore my Lifetime Pro license on a new device?

Your Lifetime Pro license is permanently tied to your store account — your Apple ID on iOS or your Google account on Android:

  1. Install TeleStudio Pro from the App Store or Google Play on your new device using the same account.
  2. Open the in-app Settings menu or the Pro upgrade screen.
  3. Tap "Restore Purchases".
  4. Apple StoreKit or Google Play will instantly validate your receipt and restore all Pro features with no additional fee.
How does Eye-Line Optimizer eliminate the "reading eyes" look?

Traditional teleprompters span the entire width of the display, causing your pupils to constantly dart left and right. TeleStudio's Eye-Line Optimizer centers and constrains the reading zone right beneath the camera lens cutout, keeping your gaze locked directly with your viewer.

How do I export 4K takes and synchronized subtitles?

In the Gallery view, select any recorded take:

  1. Tap Save to Photos to transfer the full-resolution MP4 or MOV video into your Camera Roll.
  2. Tap Export .SRT to generate time-coded subtitle files ready for Final Cut Pro, Adobe Premiere, or DaVinci Resolve.
  3. Tap Extract Audio (M4A) to pull the high-fidelity audio track from a take.
  4. Tap Share via AirDrop to send pristine raw video directly to your editing workstation.
