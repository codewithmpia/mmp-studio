Privacy Policy

Zero Data Collection

Last updated: August 29, 2026

🛡️ Summary in One Sentence

ChronoPace does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, health data, analytics, location, or telemetry. Everything happens 100% locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect

None. ChronoPace is an air-gapped utility app. It does not require user registration, account creation, or login. We do not collect your name, email address, IP address, GPS location, or device identifiers.

2. Health & Fitness Data

ChronoPace generates audio ticks, haptic vibrations, and visual pulses based on your selected steps per minute (SPM). All cadence profiles, tempo settings, and session timers are stored locally on your device using local storage (SharedPreferences) and never leave your phone.

3. Third-Party Services & Trackers

ChronoPace contains zero third-party trackers, zero advertising SDKs, and zero analytics packages (no Google Analytics, no Firebase, no Facebook SDK, etc.).

4. In-App Purchases

The optional "ChronoPace Pro" lifetime upgrade is processed entirely through Apple's App Store (StoreKit) on iOS or Google Play Billing on Android.

We do not process, receive, or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing address. All transactions are securely handled by Apple or Google under their respective privacy policies.

5. Offline Operation

ChronoPace is engineered to function completely offline. You can use all core and Pro features in Airplane Mode without an internet connection.
