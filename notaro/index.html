Engineered for Privacy & Efficiency

Everything you need to run your business

01. PRIVACY & AUTONOMY

100% Offline & Local

Your accounting data belongs strictly to you. Notaro stores company profiles, client directories, and invoices locally on your device with zero cloud server dependencies.

02. LEGAL COMPLIANCE

Cryptographic Protection

Built-in anti-tamper features including sequential non-repeating invoice numbering, SHA-256 document fingerprints, and Ed25519 digital signature QR codes on generated PDFs.

03. ZERO SUBSCRIPTIONS

Notaro Pro Lifetime (€29.99)

Notaro Pro Lifetime is a single one-time €29.99 ($29.99) purchase managed securely by Apple App Store and Google Play Store. No recurring monthly subscriptions or hidden fees.

04. CLIENT MANAGEMENT

Contacts Integration

Import client info directly from your phone's address book with a single tap. All processing executes 100% locally on your device with strict runtime permissions.

05. CATALOG & SMART ITEMS

Catalog & Item Auto-Increment

Maintain a structured catalog with customizable unit prices and tax rates. Re-selecting a catalog item automatically increments its quantity to keep invoices clean.

06. DATA PORTABILITY & EXPORTS

Accountant Sales Export & Backup

Export complete sales journal reports in CSV or PDF formats for your accountant, or create a full JSON database backup anytime to archive or transfer devices.
