02 Description of Application

FastPure is a 100% offline, private intermittent fasting utility designed to help users track fasting and eating windows (including 14:10, 16:8, 18:6, 20:4, OMAD 23:1, and custom schedules), metabolic stages (digestion, fat burning, autophagy), streaks, and local notifications.

03 Medical & Health Disclaimer

Important Medical Notice

FastPure is a time-tracking utility and DOES NOT provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Metabolic stage estimates (digestion, fat burning shift, autophagy peak, deep autophagy) are educational reference indicators only. Intermittent fasting is not suitable for everyone (including pregnant or nursing individuals, minors under 18, persons with eating disorder history, or individuals with chronic metabolic conditions). Consult a medical professional before starting any fasting routine.
