Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about how Skanzo handles your inventory.

Does Skanzo require an internet connection?
No. Skanzo is 100% offline. All barcode scanning, catalog lookups, SQLite database operations, and PDF generation run directly on your smartphone without using internet data.
What barcode formats are supported?
Skanzo supports all major 1D commercial barcodes (EAN-13, EAN-8, Code 128, Code 39, UPC-A, UPC-E) and 2D formats (QR Code).
How does the Lifetime Pro purchase work?
Skanzo Pro is a one-time non-consumable purchase (€29.99 excluding tax, or local store equivalent) tied to your Apple ID or Google Account. There are no recurring subscriptions. Prices are processed in your regional store currency, and applicable local taxes (e.g. VAT) are added at checkout. You can restore your unlock on any device using the "Restore Purchases" button in settings.
Can I import my existing product catalog?
Yes! You can import a standard CSV file containing your barcode, product name, and price fields. During scans, the app automatically fills in product names and prices.
How do I print barcode labels?
Skanzo allows you to print individual 100x62mm thermal stickers via Bluetooth or full label grid sheets via AirPrint/Mopria wireless printing.
